Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 22, 2026

Welcome to Voidrift Games. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. At Voidrift Games, we believe that creating games should be a pure and rewarding experience, and we extend that same philosophy to the protection of your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Voidrift Games ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you visit our website (voidriftgames.com) or interact with our mobile games (collectively, the "Services").

By using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide you with the best gaming experience possible.

  • Data You Provide Directly: This includes information you provide when you contact our support team, such as your name, email address, and the content of your inquiries.
  • Technical & Device Data: We may collect data such as your IP address, device type, operating system version, mobile carrier, and unique device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID, IDFA).
  • Gameplay Data: To improve our game balance, performance, and overall design, we may track general gameplay progress, levels achieved, in-game choices, and performance metrics within our games.
  • Cookies and Tracking Technologies: When you visit our website, we may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to analyze web traffic, optimize website performance, and enhance your user experience.
  • Note on Payments: All in-app purchase transactions are processed securely through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. We do not collect, process, or store your payment card information or financial details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To Improve Our Games: To analyze gameplay patterns and technical data so we can polish the quality of our projects, fix bugs, and balance mechanics.
  • To Provide Support: To respond to your comments, questions, and customer support requests efficiently.
  • To Ensure Security: To prevent fraudulent activity, cheating, and unauthorized access, and to protect our servers and your account security.
  • To Provide Updates: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we may use your contact info to send you news about our studio or new game releases. You can opt out at any time.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

As an independent studio utilizing the Cocos Creator engine, our Services may integrate third-party tools that help us run our business and improve our games. These third parties may include:

  • Analytics Providers: (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics) to understand how our games are played and used.
  • Ad Networks: (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads) to display advertisements within our games.
  • Performance Monitoring: (e.g., Firebase Crashlytics) to help us identify, diagnose, and fix game crashes and bugs.

These third-party service providers have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data. We highly encourage you to review their respective privacy policies to understand their practices.

California-Specific Disclosure: We do not "sell" or "share" your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as defined under California law (CCPA/CPRA).

4. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Voidrift Games is headquartered in Chengdu, China, and we operate globally. The information we collect may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries outside of your home country (including China and other jurisdictions where our third-party service providers or cloud servers are located). These countries may have data protection laws that are different from those of your country. By using our Services, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries outside of your country of residence.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy (e.g., for as long as your game account is active or as needed to provide you with customer support), unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. When we no longer need your data, we will securely delete or anonymize it. Anonymized data that can no longer be linked to an identifiable individual may be kept for statistical and analytical purposes.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our games are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable legal age in your jurisdiction, such as 16 in certain EU countries). If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete such information from our records.

7. Your Data Rights & Account Deletion

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the EU under GDPR, or various US state privacy laws), you may have specific rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Deletion/Erasure: Request the complete deletion of your personal data and game account.
  • Right to Object/Opt-Out: Opt-out of certain data processing activities, such as automated decision-making or targeted advertising.

To exercise any of these rights, or to submit a formal request to completely delete your game account and all associated player data, please contact us at contact@voidriftgames.com. We will verify your identity and respond to your request within the statutory timeframe (e.g., within 30 days under GDPR) in accordance with applicable laws.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. We will post the revised version on this page and update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
